# Week menu

The **Week** tab: this week's seven dinners, each a main, a side and a soup, with Western nights shaded like the printed plan.

- **Tap a night** to swap its main, side or soup (only dishes that keep fresh until that day can be picked), lock it so new plans keep it, or swap it with another day.
- **Plan this week for me** picks a new week from the recipes (locked nights stay). **Plan next week** moves on a week and avoids this week's mains. **Back to the example week** returns to the 2 November example.
- **How the ingredients are shared** lists ingredients used by more than one dish, with amounts and days, what's frozen, the dashi batches, and when the fish is bought and cooked. The rules are in `shared.js`.

Adds page 1 of the printed plan (`print.pages`).

**Requires:** week, recipes. **Uses:** household. **Saves:** nothing.
