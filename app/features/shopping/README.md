# Shopping

The **Shop** tab: the grocery list for the week, worked out from the dishes (`grocery.js`).

- **Main shop** (on the shop day): by part of the shop (Fishmonger; Meat, eggs & dairy; Vegetables & fresh; Pantry, to check before buying), then one line of staples to top up.
- **Top-up** (2 days later by default): delicate things like mushrooms and tofu, and anything that wouldn't keep from the main shop. Meat that won't keep is bought at the main shop and frozen instead (see the Prep tab).
- Amounts are added up across dishes, scaled for the household and rounded up to what you can buy ("1 kg bag (~8)").
- Tick things off; ticks are saved for each week. On shopping days, Today shows how many are ticked.

Adds page 2 of the printed plan.

**Requires:** week, recipes. **Saves:** `ticked` (by week, the last 4 weeks).
