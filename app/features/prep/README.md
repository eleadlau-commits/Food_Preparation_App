# Prep

The **Prep** tab: one card per prep session, worked out from the dishes by the week feature and grouped here (`sessions.js`).

- **Shop day** (Monday by default): dashi for the first days, marinades, portioning and freezing meat that won't keep, the aromatics jar, dressings, and anything for the first nights.
- **Top-up day** (Wednesday by default): the second dashi batch and the make-ahead sides and shaping for the rest of the week.
- Each job can be ticked off. Under each session, the reminders until the next one (move meat to the fridge the morning before, soak rice).
- Today shows today's reminders and, on a prep day, the session.

Adds pages 3 and 4 of the printed plan.

**Requires:** week. **Saves:** `done` (jobs ticked off, by week).
